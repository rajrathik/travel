Day 01 · Thu

Drive Up via Milwaukee, Green Bay & Manistique Lighthouse

~430–450 mi · ~8 hr driving + one stop
Morning CT

Depart Gurnee

Take the same north route you used: I-94 toward Milwaukee, then Green Bay, Escanaba, Manistique, and onward to Newberry. No Mackinac City and no bridge route.

Route

Milwaukee → Green Bay → Escanaba

This is the main drive spine. Keep fuel/food stops practical and short because the real scenic break is Manistique.

Afternoon ET

Manistique East Breakwater Lighthouse only outbound stop

Quick Lake Michigan harbor break before the last push to Newberry. Budget about 20–30 minutes unless the weather is perfect and you want more shoreline time.

After stop

Manistique → Newberry base

Continue north/east toward 13954 State Hwy M-28, Newberry. Settle in and keep the evening easy before the two big hiking days.

Day 02 · Fri

Tahquamenon River Trail full trek + Whitefish Point

~8 mi RT + falls
8:00a ET

Depart base

About 40 minutes north via M-123 through Paradise to the Upper Falls lot. State-park fee usually applies for nonresidents.

~8:45a

Upper Falls · 50 ft

Paved loop plus Gorge boardwalk down toward the base of the wide drop.

~10:00a

River Trail → Lower Falls

Forested trail along the Tahquamenon. Lower Falls has cascades around a midstream island. Hike back the way you came. Plan about 5–6 hours and roughly 8 miles total.

~3:30p

Whitefish Point

About 35 minutes north. Wild Lake Superior point, shipwreck history, rock-hunting beach. Good payoff and still an easy add before returning to base.

Day 03 · Sat

Chapel Loop · Pictured Rocks full loop

~10.4 mi + short beach/marsh stops · return ~7p
7:00a ET

Depart base — early & firm

About 1 hour 20 minutes west to Chapel Falls Trailhead on Chapel Road. Park by about 8:30; the lot fills and latecomers may have to walk extra road distance.

~8:30a

Chapel Loop

Clockwise keeps the cliffs in the middle: Mosquito Falls → Mosquito Beach → Lake Superior clifftop → Chapel Beach → Chapel Rock → Chapel Falls → back. Easy underfoot, just long.

After Chapel Loop

Sand Point Marsh Trail

Short, easy marsh-boardwalk style stop near Munising before leaving the Pictured Rocks area.

After marsh trail

Sand Point Beach

Quick Lake Superior beach break after the marsh trail. Keep this relaxed but not too long so you can still reach base around 7:00p.

~7:00p ET

Back to Newberry base

Return to 13954 State Hwy M-28, Newberry. This makes Day 03 a full day: Chapel Loop first, then Sand Point Marsh Trail and Sand Point Beach before heading back.

Day 04 · Sun

Drive Home via Kitch-iti-kipi, then Green Bay & Milwaukee

~430–470 mi · one return stop · gain 1 hr ET→CT
Morning ET

Depart Newberry base

Start toward Manistique / Palms Book State Park first. This is the only planned scenic stop before continuing home.

~1 hr 15m after start

Kitch-iti-kipi · Palms Book State Park only return stop

Michigan’s Big Spring. Take the self-operated observation raft over the clear spring. Budget about 45–60 minutes.

After stop

Kitch-iti-kipi → Escanaba → Green Bay → Milwaukee → Gurnee

Return using the same general way you came up. No Mackinac City, no Cut River Bridge, no McGulpin Point, and no extra detours.

Late afternoon CT

Home — Gurnee

You gain one hour crossing from Eastern to Central time, but it is still a long drive. Keep the Kitch-iti-kipi stop tight if you want a cleaner arrival.
