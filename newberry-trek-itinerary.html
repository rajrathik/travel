DAY 02 · FRI Tahquamenon River Trail full trek · ~8 mi RT + falls
8:00a
Depart base
~40 min north via M-123 through Paradise to the Upper Falls lot. State-park fee ~$11/vehicle.

Upper Falls 50 FT
Paved loop + Gorge boardwalk down toward the base of the 200-ft-wide drop.

River Trail → Lower Falls ~4 mi each way
Forested trail along the Tahquamenon. Lower Falls = cascades wrapping a midstream island; boardwalk loop. Hike back the way you came. 5–6 hr, ~8 mi total.

~35 min north. Wild Lake Superior point, shipwreck history, rock-hunting beach. Good payoff, still an easy add. Back to base by evening.
