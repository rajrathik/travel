·Sunday Breakfast — Specialty Vegetarian Spots

For your Sunday morning on May 24, 2026, staying at 775 Av. Honoré-Mercier places you within a 5- to 10-minute walk of several small, unique cafes that offer exceptional vegetarian versions of traditional Québécois breakfast foods. These locations are known for their intimate atmosphere and unique twists on local flavors:

Bistro l'Orygine

Located in the heart of Old Quebec, this bistro specializes in refined, plant-centric cuisine using local organic ingredients. Their brunch is highly regarded for its creative vegetable-forward dishes that offer a modern, elevated take on traditional flavors.

Le Billig

A charming, small creperie on Rue Saint-Jean known for its authentic buckwheat galettes. They offer a variety of savory vegetarian crepes filled with local cheeses and fresh seasonal vegetables, providing a cozy and unique Québécois-Breton experience.

La Bûche

This restaurant offers a festive, sugar-shack-inspired atmosphere with a rustic decor. While famous for traditional meats, they provide hearty vegetarian options like "Poor Man's Pudding" (Pouding chômeur) and creative veggie-based omelets.

·Unique Foods to Try (Vegetarian Style)

When visiting these spots, look for these specific unique items to get a true taste of the region:

  • Pouding Chômeur (Poor Man's Pudding)A classic Québécois dessert often served at breakfast, consisting of a white cake batter baked in maple syrup or caramel.
  • Savory Buckwheat GalettesA traditional staple in Quebec, these are gluten-free crepes made with buckwheat flour. Ask for them filled with local Oka or Migneron cheese and seasonal vegetables.
  • Maple Taffy on SnowEven in May, some traditional spots like La Bûche may offer maple taffy, where hot maple syrup is poured onto snow and rolled onto a stick.
  • Fèves au Lard (Vegetarian Version)Look for "Maple Baked Beans" that are prepared without the traditional lard or salt pork. They are a staple of a "full Quebec breakfast" and are richly flavored with local maple syrup.
